About

This platform supports the university's international cooperation projects. It helps researchers declare their project intentions, submit their drafts, request support and follow their proposals up to submission, while giving managers the tools to review proposals and follow decisions.

What you can do

  • Declare a project and choose the program and call
  • Submit a draft and describe your support needs
  • Track the status of your proposal
  • Contact the cooperation office for help
